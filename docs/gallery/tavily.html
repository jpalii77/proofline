Proofline

Tavily results are part of the proof, not just the starting point.

1. Discovery

One search for the name and city. The model may take a domain or phone only from these results.

2. Whose site is it?

Search results must tie a domain to the business and its city. Before “no website”, a second search and the listing’s own link.

3. Closed or moved?

“Permanently closed” counts only if the result names the business. “We moved” is not a closure.
